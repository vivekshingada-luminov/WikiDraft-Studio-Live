Live view for your collaborator

One-time setup, about ten minutes. Your text is sent to a free Firebase database that you own, and your collaborator reads it from there.

  1. Go to console.firebase.google.com, create a project, then Build → Realtime Database → Create database (locked mode is fine).
  2. Open the database's Rules tab, paste the rules below, and press Publish.
  3. Copy the database URL shown at the top of the Data tab and paste it here.

Database connected. Starting a session sends your draft to it and keeps it updated as you write.

Anyone who has the link or code can read the draft while the session runs. Stopping the session deletes it from the database.

Your session is live. Send either of these to your collaborator.

With the code, they open their own copy of this file, press Watch, and paste it in.
